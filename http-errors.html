HTTP errors

Encountering HTTP errors? Here's what they mean and how to address them.

403: Forbidden404: Not Found429: Too Many Requests5xx: Server Errors1006, 1007, 1008, 1106: Access Denied1020: Access Denied

403: Forbidden

The source is protected or has banned your IP. Open the source in WebView to confirm it loads there.

404: Not Found

The source is down or the series was removed. Verify in WebView, then consider switching sources.

429: Too Many Requests

The source temporarily banned your IP after too many fast downloads or reads. Wait a while before retrying.

5xx: Server Errors

Something is wrong on the source's own server, for example 500 or 502. Check the source in WebView and retry later.

1006, 1007, 1008, 1106: Access Denied

A temporary IP ban by the source. Wait it out, then retry.

1020: Access Denied

You tripped a firewall rule set by the site owner, who may have raised protection. Try again later or use another source.
